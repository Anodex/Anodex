Then rank the retrofit options by how much benefit they deliver relative to their installed cost and disruption, for a house that already has decent attic insulation, original single-pane windows, a 20-year-old gas furnace, and existing ductwork. Be specific about what should be done first and why, and say plainly which measures are not worth it for a house in this condition.
