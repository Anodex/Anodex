Explain what actually caused the collapse of the Late Bronze Age civilisations of the eastern Mediterranean around 1200-1150 BC, and weigh the competing explanations against each other. Cover: what the archaeological destruction layers at sites such as Ugarit, Hattusa, Mycenae and Pylos actually show and how securely they are dated, what the surviving textual evidence (the Amarna and Ugarit letters, Egyptian inscriptions at Medinet Habu, Hittite correspondence) says and how much weight it can bear, and the main proposed causes: the Sea Peoples, drought and climate change, earthquake storms, systems collapse of palace economies, disease, and internal social upheaval. For each explanation, say what evidence supports it, what evidence tells against it, and which scholars advance it. Be explicit about where the evidence is genuinely ambiguous, where the popular account outruns what the sources support, and which questions the current evidence simply cannot settle.
