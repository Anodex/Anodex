Assess the current evidence on creatine monohydrate supplementation in adults over 60, and give a clear recommendation. Cover: what randomised controlled trials and meta-analyses actually show for muscle mass, strength and physical function; whether there is credible evidence for cognitive effects; what dose and loading protocols were tested and which are supported; what is known about kidney function, hydration and interactions in older adults, including in people with existing renal impairment; how effect sizes compare with resistance training alone and with the combination; and where the evidence base is weak, underpowered, or funded by manufacturers. Then state plainly whether a healthy 68-year-old should take it, at what dose, what should be checked first, and who should not take it. Distinguish clearly between what is well established, what is plausible but unproven, and what is marketing.
