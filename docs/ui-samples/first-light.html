First light

The very first reply of a brand-new chat is the conversation coming alive — so it arrives with light, once. As the thinking indicator hands off to real words, the sparkle flares and dissolves, and a faint band of accent light passes over the opening text (~800ms). Every reply after that arrives plain: dawn happens once per conversation.

Integration: MessageList passes a firstLight flag to MessageBubble when the message is the conversation's first assistant turn and it is streaming live (thinking → first content chunk in this mount). The flag adds the flare to the indicator's sparkle and mounts the one-shot light band over the row; history renders and every later reply omit it. Honors prefers-reduced-motion. Theme tokens only — verify both modes with the toggle.
