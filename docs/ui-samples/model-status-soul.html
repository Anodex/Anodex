Anodex / UI sample

Model status — loading & completed motion

Three treatments for the status row at the bottom of the left panel (ModelStatusMenu.tsx), replacing the plain pulsing-halo dot with something that carries the same visual language as the startup hyperspace jump and the AI & Models starfield download bar. Each card loops on its own: loading → arrival flourish → ready hold → repeat.

Concept 1

Comet Trail

A tight cyan → blue → violet comet orbits the dot while the model loads. On completion it flares once and settles green. Continuous and energetic — a new motif, not reused from elsewhere in the app.
